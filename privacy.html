Privacy Policy

Thirudan Police (திருடன் போலீஸ்) — Last updated: September 10, 2026

Thirudan Police is a multiplayer party game. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Player nickname — the name you type in when creating or joining a game room. This is visible to other players in the same room.
  • Anonymous account identifier — the app signs you in anonymously (no email, phone number, or password) so your session can be recognized. This identifier is not linked to your real identity.
  • Gameplay data — room codes, role assignments, guesses, and scores generated while you play, stored so the game can sync between players in real time.
  • Locally stored data — your device saves a short list of recently visited room codes and nicknames on your device only, so you don't have to retype them. This never leaves your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No email address, phone number, or precise location
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs

How Information Is Used

Nicknames and gameplay data are used solely to run the game — matching players in the same room, tracking roles and scores, and showing results. This data is processed by our backend provider, Supabase, and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Retention

Room and gameplay data is temporary and tied to the room's lifecycle. Locally stored recent-room history stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Thirudan Police does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond a self-chosen in-game nickname, which is not required to be a real name.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
